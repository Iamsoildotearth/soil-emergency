Soil Emergency

Our food future is dying beneath our feet. We must act now to restore the living soil.

⚠️ Britain is losing topsoil faster than it can be replaced.
This silent crisis threatens our food, wildlife, and climate. Come and talk to us at the Festival of Nature stall.

The Crisis

Industrial farming and chemical overuse have degraded our soils to a critical level. Healthy soil is the foundation of everything we eat.

Our Mission

We are calling for urgent government action and widespread regenerative farming to rebuild Britain’s soil health.
