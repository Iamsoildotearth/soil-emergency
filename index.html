Soil Emergency

Protecting Future Harvests

Healthy soils underpin food security. This campaign calls for urgent action to restore soil health, establish national monitoring and ensure long-term accountability for one of the UK's most important natural assets.

Why This Matters

Healthy soils support food production, biodiversity, water regulation and climate resilience. Without healthy soils, agricultural productivity and food security become increasingly vulnerable.

The European Union has introduced a soil monitoring and resilience framework. Many citizens are now asking what equivalent action should be taken within the United Kingdom.

Questions We Are Asking

  1. When will a statutory Soil Health Strategy be introduced with measurable targets?
  2. When will soil health be formally recognised as a strategic component of food security policy?
  3. When will an independent national soil survey establish an up-to-date baseline?
  4. When will national soil health targets be established?
  5. When will policy ambition be aligned with leading international soil restoration programmes?
  6. When will a cross-departmental Soil Emergency Working Group be established?
  7. When will a framework for measuring, independently verifying and publicly reporting progress be introduced?
